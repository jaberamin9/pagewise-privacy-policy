Pagewise Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026 · App: Pagewise (Android, package com.softlabs.pagewise)

Pagewise is a PDF reader and toolkit for Android. This policy explains what information Pagewise handles, why, and the choices you have. "We" and "us" mean the developer of Pagewise.

In short
  • Your documents never leave your phone. Reading, editing, converting, compressing and text recognition (OCR) all run on your device.
  • There is no account. We don't collect your name, email address, contacts or precise location.
  • Pagewise is free and shows ads from Google (AdMob), which use your device's advertising ID. Where the law requires it, you're asked for consent first.
  • Pagewise sends anonymous usage counts (like app launches and tool runs) with a random ID made on your phone, never your files or anything you type.

1. Your documents and files

  • Pagewise opens only the files you choose in Android's file picker. It does not scan or browse your storage on its own.
  • All document processing (opening, editing, signing, merging, splitting, compressing, converting, recognising text) happens on your phone. Your documents, their contents and their file names are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • To show your recent documents, history, favourites and settings, Pagewise stores that information inside the app on your phone. It is not sent anywhere and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Temporary working files are kept inside the app and deleted automatically. You can also remove them in Settings → Clear temporary files.

3. Advertising

Pagewise is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, device model and operating system, your approximate location based on your IP address, and how you interact with ads. Ads in Pagewise are limited to content suitable for a general audience, and ads never have access to your documents.

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland (and elsewhere where the law requires it), Pagewise uses Google's consent tool to ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Your advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your phone's settings (for example Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone). Without it, ads are not personalised.
  • Optional ad-free time: Settings offers a short video ad that, if you watch it to the end, turns ads off for a while. Watching it is entirely your choice.

Learn more about how Google uses information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which features are used and to find problems, Pagewise counts events such as app launches, tool runs, tool errors, documents opened, edits saved and ads shown. With these counts it sends:

  • a random install ID created by Pagewise on your phone when you first open it. It is not derived from your phone's hardware, your advertising ID or any account, and it is not linked to you;
  • the version of Pagewise you're using.

Pagewise never sends your files, file names, document contents or anything you type. The counts are combined into daily totals that we look at only in aggregate.

5. App settings downloads

Pagewise downloads its settings from our server, for example which ads to show, whether a newer version is available, and the texts of this policy and our terms. These requests contain no personal information. Like any connection to the internet, they reveal your IP address to the server; our hosting provider may keep it briefly in its security logs, and we do not use it to identify you.

6. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to show ads, download app settings and send anonymous usage counts. Documents are never uploaded.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.
  • Vibration: for small haptic feedback in the editor.
  • Files: Pagewise opens and saves files through Android's file picker, so it sees only the files and folders you choose.

7. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell your information. We don't share it with anyone except these service providers, who act for the purposes described above:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): advertising. See Advertising.
  • Supabase (Supabase, Inc.): hosts our app settings and stores the anonymous usage counts. Supabase Privacy Policy.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, ourselves or others.

8. Retention and deletion

  • On your phone: your recent documents, history and settings stay in the app until you remove them, clear the app's data or uninstall Pagewise.
  • Usage statistics: kept as daily records for as long as they are useful for improving the app. Because the install ID is random and not linked to you, we cannot look up the records of a particular person. Uninstalling Pagewise (or clearing its data) deletes the ID from your phone, and nothing more is sent from that install.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google under its own policies.

9. Security

Your documents stay on your phone, protected by Android's app sandbox. Information sent over the internet is encrypted with HTTPS, and access to our stored usage statistics is restricted to the app's administrators. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect the information we hold.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Change your ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy choices (where available), and reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Delete the data Pagewise keeps on your phone by clearing the app's data in Android settings, or by uninstalling it.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA/UK, or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us to exercise them.

11. Children

Pagewise is a general document tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Pagewise, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Pagewise changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date, and the current version is always available in the app under Settings → Privacy policy.
